UNESCO Cultural Inscription

In December 2021, UNESCO officially inscribed 'Durga Puja in Kolkata' on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. Inscribed not merely as a sacred religious observance, but as the world's largest public art installation and an unparalleled celebration of craftsmanship, street gastronomy, and collective unity.

Cultural Highlights
  • Inscribed on December 15, 2021, by the UNESCO Intergovernmental Committee in Paris
  • Celebrated as a month-long democratic festival embracing all communities without barrier
  • Seamlessly synthesizes ancient clay sculpting traditions with avant-garde contemporary art

Maha Ashtami: Kumari Puja & Sandhi Puja

108 Lotus Offering & Peak Divine Energy

Young girls are worshipped as living incarnations of the Goddess in Kumari Puja. At the stroke of midnight during Sandhi Puja (the junction of Ashtami and Navami), 108 blue lotuses and 108 oil lamps are offered in 24 minutes.
