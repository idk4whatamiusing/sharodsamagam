Red Road Carnival & Immersion Guide

Kolkata's iconic post-Dashami Red Road Mega Carnival parade and the historic Hooghly River immersion ghats (বিসর্জন ও রেড রোড কার্নিভাল).

Kolkata UNESCO Durga Puja Red Road Mega Carnival

Organized by the Government of West Bengal, the Red Road Carnival is a world-renowned grand spectacle celebrating Kolkata's UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage status. Over 90-100 top award-winning Durga Puja committees parade down Red Road with spectacular tableaux, live dhaki ensembles, Chhau & Baul dancers, and artistic cultural floats before heading to the Ganges immersion ghats.

24th October, 2026 (Saturday) Red Road (Maidan Area)Pass-based & Open Viewing
Pass & Entry Information:

Passes issued by West Bengal Tourism & Kolkata Police. Dedicated VIP, Diplomatic, and General Public viewing galleries along Red Road.

Vijaya Dashami Rituals & Traditions

The sacred customs observed across Kolkata before Ma Durga departs for Kailash

Sindoor Khela (সিঁদুর খেলা)

Married women apply vermilion on Goddess Durga's forehead and feet, offering sweets before smearing sindoor among one another in a vibrant celebration of womanhood.

Devi Boron & Kulaari

The farewell ritual where women touch betel leaves to Maa Durga's face and feed her sweets and betel nuts, wishing her a safe journey back to Mount Kailash.

Eco-Friendly Immersion

Kolkata Municipal Corporation uses hydraulic cranes and winch mechanisms to retrieve idols within minutes of immersion, keeping the holy Hooghly River clean.
